## Invoicing habits that get you paid faster

- **Number sequentially** and never reuse a number. Accountants and tax authorities expect an unbroken series. Start with something like INV-0001.
- **Put a clear due date,** not just "payment terms". "Due 15 November 2026" is harder to ignore than "Net 30".
- **Describe items specifically.** "Website redesign, homepage and 4 inner pages, October 2026" beats "Design work".
- **Include payment details** in the notes: bank account, UPI ID or a payment link.
- **Send it promptly.** Invoices sent the day work finishes get paid sooner than ones sent weeks later.
- **Record partial payments** in the amount-paid field so the balance due is always clear.
