Every January or February, HR sends the reminder: submit rent receipts for your HRA exemption or more tax gets deducted from your last few salaries. Then begins the hunt for a landlord who'll sign twelve receipts. ## Steps

1. Enter who paid the rent and who received it, and the property address.
2. Pick one period, or **Every month in a range** for one receipt per month, for example April 2026 to March 2027.
3. Add a signature if you like (drawn or uploaded).
4. Press **Download PDF** or **Print**.

## What each receipt contains

- Its own receipt number, counting up from the one you type.
- Date, payer, property and the month.
- Rent in figures and in words (Indian wording uses lakh and crore).
- Landlord's PAN, only if you type one.
- A signature above the "Received by" line, if you added one.

A financial year range gives you 12 receipts, one per page, each with its month, number and rent.

## The revenue stamp rule

When rent is paid in **cash** and is over ₹5,000, a revenue stamp is usually needed on the receipt, with the landlord signing across it. The page reminds you and leaves a stamp box only in that case. Rent paid by bank transfer or UPI doesn't trigger the reminder, and a bank record of payments is good supporting evidence anyway.

## Landlord's PAN

The PAN line is optional and left off if blank. Many employers ask for the landlord's PAN when yearly rent is over ₹1 lakh, so check your company's policy. If the landlord doesn't have a PAN, employers typically want a declaration from them instead. HR will tell you the format.

## HRA basics (and the regime question)

HRA exemption is only available in the **old tax regime**. If you've chosen the new regime, rent receipts won't reduce your tax, though some employers still collect them. Compare both regimes with [Income tax (India)](https://bestlatestapps.com/tools/income-tax-calculator/) before deciding.

## Tips so HR doesn't bounce them

- **Match the address** to your rental agreement exactly.
- **Use consistent amounts.** If rent changed mid-year, make two ranges with different amounts.
- **Keep the agreement and bank statements** in case of questions.
- **Get the landlord to sign** each page, even if you add a digital signature, unless your employer accepts digital ones.
- **Rent paid to parents** is allowed if they own the property and declare the income, but keep proof of payment.
