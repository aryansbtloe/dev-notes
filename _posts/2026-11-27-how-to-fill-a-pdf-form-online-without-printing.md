School admission forms, bank KYC updates, visa checklists: plenty of these come as PDFs with real form fields built in. On a laptop without Acrobat, or on a phone, actually typing into them can be oddly hard. [Fill PDF form](https://bestlatestapps.com/tools/fill-pdf-form/) reads the fields the PDF already contains, lays them out as ordinary inputs, and writes your answers back into the file.

## Which fields it understands

It works with standard PDF form fields (AcroForm):

- text boxes
- checkboxes (yes or no)
- dropdowns and option lists
- radio button groups

Choice fields only offer the options the PDF author defined. A field with no name is skipped. And it won't invent fields: if a page just looks like a form (lines and boxes printed on it) but has no actual fields, there's nothing for this tool to fill.

## How to fill a form

1. Open the tool and choose the PDF.
2. Press **Read fields**. The fields appear as a list of inputs.
3. Type and tick your answers.
4. Press **Save filled PDF**.
5. Open the download and check each page.

## Example: a school admission form

A school emails a four-page admission PDF. Read fields shows forty-odd entries: student name, date of birth, parents' names, a dropdown for class, checkboxes for transport and lunch. Fill them in one place instead of hunting around the page, save, and email it back. Because the fields remain editable in the download, the school's office can correct a typo without asking you to redo it.

## When you see no fields

That's the most common question, and the answer is almost always that the PDF is "flat". Someone designed a form and exported it without fields, or scanned a paper form. Two options:

- Use [Edit PDF](https://bestlatestapps.com/tools/edit-pdf/) to place text wherever you need it on the page.
- If the form is a scan, consider printing, filling by hand and making a clean copy with [Scan to PDF](https://bestlatestapps.com/tools/scan-to-pdf/).

## Tips and pitfalls

- **Dates:** fields don't always tell you the format. Look at the printed label next to the field (DD/MM/YYYY versus MM/DD/YYYY) before typing.
- **Long answers:** a text field may visually cut off long text in some viewers. Keep answers concise or check the saved PDF.
- **Signatures:** a text field can hold your typed name, but this page doesn't create certificate-based digital signatures. For a visible signature, use [Sign PDF](https://bestlatestapps.com/tools/sign-pdf/) after filling.
- **Locking the answers:** the values stay editable. If you want the form frozen so nobody changes your answers, flatten it after filling.
- **Locked PDFs:** if the form asks for a password to open, unlock it with the password you have first.

## How to tell if a PDF is fillable

Open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. If clicking a blank line puts a cursor in a box, or the fields glow blue or grey, it has real fields and this tool will find them. If clicking does nothing, it's flat. Government forms downloaded from official portals are often fillable; forms that someone scanned and emailed rarely are. Knowing this before you start saves a frustrating round of "why can't I type here?".

## Privacy

Forms carry exactly the kind of information you don't want floating around: dates of birth, addresses, ID numbers. The tool page says the fields are read and filled in the tab and the PDF isn't uploaded. There are Google ads on the page, as across the site.

## FAQ

**Why do I see no fields?**
The PDF doesn't contain fillable fields, only printed lines. Use Edit PDF to type on it.

**Can it add a new box?**
No, it only fills fields that already exist.

**Are the values locked after saving?**
No, they remain editable.

**Is the PDF uploaded?**
No, everything happens in the tab.
